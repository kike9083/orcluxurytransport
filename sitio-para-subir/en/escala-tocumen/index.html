Tocumen layover tour · 5-6 hours

What can I do on a layover in Panama?

With a 5- to 6-hour layover at Tocumen International Airport, a private tour of the Panama Canal and Casco Antiguo fits. The driver waits in the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name, your luggage travels with you in the vehicle, and the return is timed to your boarding time. The typical plan: a 40-60 minute drive to the Miraflores Locks, about ninety minutes between the viewing platform and the visitor center, an hour and a half in Casco Antiguo, and a direct ride back to the airport. The price is fixed and confirmed over WhatsApp (+507 6802-6916) before departure.

Questions

Questions layover travelers ask

Do I have enough time on a 4-hour layover?

We recommend a minimum 5-hour layover for the full tour. With 4 hours, a short Canal visit may still be possible depending on your flight: send us your schedule and we will honestly tell you whether it fits.

What if my flight lands late?

We track your flight and the driver waits at no extra charge: 60 minutes of free waiting on international arrivals.

Where do I meet the driver?

In the arrivals hall at Tocumen, holding a sign with your name, right after immigration and customs.

Can I do the tour with my luggage?

Yes. The vehicle and driver are private: your luggage travels with you at all times, with no lockers or extra steps.

How much does the layover tour cost?

The price is fixed and depends on the length of your layover. Send your flight details on WhatsApp to +507 6802-6916 and you receive the final price within minutes.
