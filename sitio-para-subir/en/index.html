Transportation rates in Panama

How much does an airport transfer from Tocumen (TO) cost?

A private transfer from Tocumen International Airport (TO) to Panama City starts at USD 32 in a sedan for up to 3 passengers and USD 45 in a minivan for up to 6. The price is fixed: it is confirmed on WhatsApp before departure and includes tolls, parking, flight tracking and 60 minutes of free waiting time on international arrivals. There are no surcharges by schedule: early mornings, weekends and holidays cost the same. The drive takes 25 to 45 minutes depending on traffic, over about 24 kilometres. Transfers from Albrook Airport inside the city start at USD 25. To book, send the date, time, flight number and destination on WhatsApp and you will receive the final price in minutes.

How long does it take from Tocumen to the city?

It is about 24 kilometres: 25 to 45 minutes depending on traffic. Rush hours (12:00-14:00 and 17:00-19:00) are the busiest. For an international departure we recommend leaving the hotel three hours ahead; two hours is usually enough for domestic flights.

Frequently asked questions

Will you wait if my flight is delayed?

Yes. We track your flight and the driver waits in the arrivals hall at no extra charge, up to 60 minutes after you land.

Where do I meet the driver at Tocumen?

The driver waits in the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name, right after customs. If you prefer another pickup point, we can agree on it on WhatsApp.

How do I pay for the transfer?

There are no online payments or deposits: you confirm the fixed price on WhatsApp and pay the driver directly in US dollars or Balboas, in cash or as agreed.

Do you have child car seats?

Yes. Tell us the children's ages when booking and we prepare the appropriate seat at no extra charge, subject to availability.

How many suitcases fit?

The sedan fits 2 or 3 large suitcases; the seven-seater minivan fits 4 to 6 depending on passengers. If you travel with more luggage, we will clarify when confirming.

Do the drivers speak English?

Yes. The team serves in Spanish and English, and the WhatsApp coordination is bilingual, 24/7.

Is a tip included?

The fixed price includes the vehicle, driver, tolls and parking. Tipping is not mandatory; it is entirely your call.

Can I hire the vehicle by the hour?

Yes. Hourly service starts at USD 35 with a two-hour minimum, and half-day or full-day private service includes the stops you choose.
