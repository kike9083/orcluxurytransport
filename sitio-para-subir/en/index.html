FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will you wait for me if my flight is delayed?

Yes. We track your flight status in real time and the driver waits for you at arrivals, at no extra charge, up to 60 minutes after landing.

Where do I meet the driver at Tocumen?

At the arrivals hall, holding a sign with your name, right after you clear immigration and customs. It is a Meet & Greet service included on all arrivals.

How do I pay for the transfer?

No online payments or deposits: you confirm the closed price via WhatsApp and pay the driver in US dollars or balboas. We can also arrange credit cards, PayPal or a wireless POS terminal if you prefer.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the service. Within the last 24 hours, message us anyway: we will look for the fairest solution for both sides.

Do you provide child safety seats?

Yes. When booking, mention your children's ages and we will prepare the right seat at no extra charge, subject to availability.

How many suitcases fit?

The sedan fits 2 or 3 large suitcases; the minivan fits 4 to 6 depending on the number of passengers. If you are carrying more luggage, we will clarify it when confirming.

Do your drivers speak English?

Yes. Our team assists in both Spanish and English, and WhatsApp coordination is bilingual too, 24/7.
